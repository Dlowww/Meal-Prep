How to get a free key
  1. Open spoonacular.com/food-api/console.
  2. Tap Sign up, then enter your email and a password. Choose the Free plan. No card is needed.
  3. Confirm your email using the link Spoonacular sends you.
  4. Log in, then open Profile & API Key in the menu.
  5. Tap Show / Hide API Key, copy the long code, and paste it in the box above.
